## Journey Builder

*Orchestrate multi-step, cross-channel customer journeys.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Journey Builder** is Marketing Cloud's tool to design **automated, multi-step customer journeys** across channels (email, SMS, push, ads) — triggered by events or audiences, with decision splits, waits, and actions.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Entry source + activities (message/split/wait/update).
- Runs on Contacts + data extensions; versioned; goals/exit.

## Email Studio

*Build, personalize, and send email campaigns.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Email Studio** is Marketing Cloud's tool to create, personalize, test, and send **email campaigns** to subscriber lists or data extensions — with templates, dynamic content, and deliverability tooling.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Data extensions + AMPscript/dynamic content; A/B + Einstein.
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC + IP warmup + reputation; consent/tracking.

## Mobile Studio

*SMS, MMS, and push messaging at scale.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Mobile Studio** handles **mobile messaging** — SMS/MMS (MobileConnect), push notifications (MobilePush), and group messaging (GroupConnect) — integrated into journeys for cross-channel engagement.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- MobileConnect (SMS) / MobilePush (SDK) / GroupConnect (WhatsApp).
- Strict separate consent; short codes/10DLC; journey integration.

## Automation Studio

*Schedule and orchestrate data/messaging workflows.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Automation Studio** automates **data and messaging workflows** on a schedule or trigger — imports, SQL queries, data extracts, file transfers, and sends — for the behind-the-scenes data operations powering campaigns.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Activities (File/Import/SQL/Extract/Send/Script); scheduled/file-drop.
- SQL Query for segmentation; ETL backbone; complements journeys.

## Contact Builder

*Define the contact data model and relationships.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Contact Builder** defines Marketing Cloud's **contact data model** — how contacts relate to data extensions and attributes — so personalization and journeys can reference the right data for each person.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Contact Key identity; Data Designer relationships; attribute groups.
- Channel addresses; GDPR deletion; foundation for personalization.

## Data Extensions

*Relational tables storing audience and campaign data.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Data Extensions** are Marketing Cloud's **relational tables** that store subscriber and campaign data — used as send audiences, personalization sources, and the targets of imports and SQL queries.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Typed fields + primary key + sendable; Standard/Filtered/SQL types.
- Relational (Data Designer); retention; scales to millions.

## Personalization

*Tailor content to each individual at send/runtime.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Personalization** tailors message content to each recipient — using their data via **AMPscript**, personalization strings, dynamic content, and (with Personalization/Interaction Studio) real-time behavioral targeting.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- AMPscript/dynamic content (batch) + MC Personalization/Einstein (real-time).
- Depends on clean data model; performance + privacy aware.
