## Subscription Management

*Manage recurring products across the lifecycle.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Subscription Management** handles recurring products across their lifecycle — initial sale, amendments, renewals, and cancellations — tracking what each customer has and the recurring revenue (MRR/ARR) it generates.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Subscription records (CPQ/RLM); amend/renew/cancel; proration.
- Feeds billing + MRR/ARR; integrity via flows not manual edits.

## Billing

*Turn subscriptions/orders into invoices and payments.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Billing** (Salesforce Billing / Revenue Cloud) converts orders and subscriptions into **invoices, payments, and credit** — generating billing schedules, prorating, taxing, and collecting cash.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Order → invoices per schedule; proration; tax; payments; credits.
- Feeds revenue recognition/ERP; native (RLM) vs external (Zuora).

## Revenue Recognition

*Recognize revenue per accounting standards over time.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Revenue Recognition** determines **when and how much revenue** to record, spreading it over the period it's earned (per ASC 606 / IFRS 15) — distinct from when you invoice or collect cash.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Revenue schedules over service period; deferred revenue; ASC 606.
- Obligation allocation; methods (ratable/point/milestone); GL/ERP post.
