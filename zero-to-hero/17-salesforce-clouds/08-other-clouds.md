## Health Cloud Care Plans

*Coordinate patient care with goals and tasks.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Care Plans** in Health Cloud organize a patient's **health goals, problems, and tasks** into a coordinated plan — letting care teams track progress toward better outcomes.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Care Plan + Goals/Problems/Tasks; care team; condition templates.
- Person Account model; FHIR/EHR; HIPAA/Shield; timeline tracking.

## FSC Household

*Model family/household relationships in finance.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Households** in Financial Services Cloud (FSC) group related individuals (family members) so advisors see the whole **household's relationships, accounts, and rollups** together.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Household = Account + ACR + reciprocal roles; account rollups.
- Relationship groups; advisor console; sharing; core-banking integration.

## FSC Financial Account

*Represent banking/investment accounts in FSC.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Financial Accounts** in FSC represent a client's **banking, investment, and insurance accounts** (checking, brokerage, policies) — linked to clients and households for a complete financial picture.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Financial Account + Roles + Holdings + transactions; household links.
- Core-banking sync; replicate vs virtualize by volume; Shield/audit.

## Manufacturing Sales Agreements

*Track committed volumes and run-rate business.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Sales Agreements** in Manufacturing Cloud track **committed/forecasted volumes and revenue** over time for run-rate business — giving manufacturers visibility into negotiated long-term commitments.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Schedules (planned vs actual) per product/period; ERP actuals.
- Account-Based Forecasting; run-rate vs one-off; large data.
