## Apps

*A named collection of tabs/items giving users a focused workspace (Lightning apps).*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

An **app** in Salesforce is a bundle of tabs and items organized for a job — like a "Sales" app with Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities tabs. Users switch apps from the App Launcher to get a focused workspace.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Custom app counts are limited by edition.
- Each app carries its own navigation, utility bar and page assignments.
- App visibility is controlled per profile or permission set.

## Branding

*App-level visual identity — logo, color, theme — for recognition and white-labeling.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Branding** lets you set an app's **logo, primary color, and theme** so it matches your company (or a specific app's) identity. Users instantly recognize which app they're in.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Per-app logo/color/theme; org default via Themes & Branding; full theming only in Experience Cloud; assets as metadata.

## Utility Items

*Persistent footer tools (utility bar) available across the whole app — notes, history, Omni-Channel, custom LWC.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Utility items** live in the **utility bar** — a footer that stays visible everywhere in an app. Click one to pop open a tool (Notes, Recent Items, History, Omni-Channel) without leaving your record.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Utility bar components are always loaded, so heavy ones slow every page in the app.
- Practical limit of a handful of items before the bar becomes unusable.
- Not available in the mobile app.
