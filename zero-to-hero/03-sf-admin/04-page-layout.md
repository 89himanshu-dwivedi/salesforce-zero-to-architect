## Sections

*Group related fields into labeled blocks on a layout for readable, organized records.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Sections** divide a page layout into labeled groups of fields (e.g., "Address Information", "System Details"). They make records readable instead of one long field list.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Layout required/read-only is UI-only; 1–2 columns; related lists/buttons still on layout; Dynamic Forms adds visibility rules.

## Buttons

*Actions on a record/list — standard, custom, and Lightning quick actions that drive workflows.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Buttons** trigger actions on records or list views — standard ones (New, Edit, Delete) and **custom** ones you build (e.g., "Create Renewal", "Send to ERP"). They put workflows one click away.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- No JS buttons in Lightning; quick actions for modern UX; visibility via layout/Dynamic Actions; action count affects clutter.

## Related Lists

*Show child/related records on a parent page — contacts on an account, cases on a contact.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Related lists** display records connected to the one you're viewing — like the Contacts, Opportunities, and Cases that belong to an Account. They let you see and act on related data without leaving the page.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Need a relationship; column/visible-row caps; performance with high child volume; Enhanced list for more columns.
