## Groups

*Chatter collaboration spaces (public/private/unlisted) for teams to share posts, files, polls.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Chatter Groups** are collaboration spaces where people post updates, ask questions, share files, and run polls around a topic or team — like a "Deal War Room" or "Product Announcements" group.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Public/Private/Unlisted/broadcast types; visibility by membership (not sharing); external members need "allow customers"/Experience Cloud.

## Feed Tracking

*Configure which record field changes post automatically to the record's Chatter feed.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Feed tracking** decides which **field changes** on a record automatically appear in its Chatter feed — so when, say, an Opportunity's Stage or Amount changes, everyone following sees a post about it.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Maximum 20 tracked fields per object.
- Feed items consume data storage and grow quickly on high-churn fields.
- Not available on every object.
- Feed tracking does not create field history - they are separate features.

## Mentions

*@-mention people or groups in posts/comments to notify and pull them into a conversation.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**@Mentions** let you tag a person or group in a Chatter post or comment (type `@Name`). They get a **notification** and the conversation is pulled to their attention — like tagging someone on social media.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Notify users/groups only; respect visibility/sharing; honor each user's notification prefs; not an access grant.
