## Compact Layout

*Defines the key fields shown in the highlights panel and mobile/hover cards.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A **compact layout** picks the handful of **most important fields** to show at the top of a record (the highlights panel), in hover cards, and on mobile. It's the "at a glance" summary of a record.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Limited number of fields; per-record-type assignable; drives mobile/hover/highlights; system default until customized.

## Dynamic Forms

*Field-level page configuration with visibility rules — replaces the monolithic page layout for fields.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Dynamic Forms** let you place *individual fields and sections* directly on the Lightning record page and **show/hide them by rules** — instead of one rigid page layout for everyone. The page adapts to the record and user.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Per Lightning page; fields only (layout keeps related lists/buttons/mobile cards); required/read-only UI-only; assign pages by app/RT/profile.

## Dynamic Actions

*Show/hide record-page actions (buttons) conditionally based on record data or user.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Dynamic Actions** let you control *which buttons* appear on a Lightning record page using **rules** — show "Escalate" only on open high-priority cases, or "Approve" only to managers. Actions adapt to context.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Per Lightning page; presentation-only (not security); visibility by value/RT/device/permission; assign pages by persona.
