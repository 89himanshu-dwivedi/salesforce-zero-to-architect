## Assignment Rules

*Auto-route new cases/leads to the right user or queue based on criteria.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Assignment Rules** automatically route a new **case** (or lead) to the right owner — a user or a **queue** — based on criteria like type, origin, or region. No manual triage needed.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- One active rule/object; first-match entries; header needed for API/Flow; pair with Omni-Channel for advanced routing.

## Escalation Rules

*Automatically escalate cases that breach time/criteria — reassign and notify.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Escalation Rules** automatically **escalate** cases that aren't handled in time — e.g., a high-priority case open over 2 hours gets reassigned to a senior queue and managers are notified.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Time-based on age/last-modified; uses Business Hours/holidays; reassign/notify actions; Entitlements for formal SLAs.

## Auto Response Rules

*Send an immediate templated acknowledgement to the contact when a case/lead is created.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Auto-Response Rules** send an **instant email reply** to the customer when a case (or lead) comes in — "We've received your request, ticket #12345" — based on criteria, with no agent effort.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- One active rule/object; first-match; single response per record; org-wide from-address; email limits/deliverability.
