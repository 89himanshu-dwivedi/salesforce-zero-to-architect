## Stakeholder Management

*Align diverse stakeholders behind a solution.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Stakeholder management** is identifying everyone affected by a project, understanding their needs and influence, and keeping them **aligned and informed** so the solution succeeds.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Power/interest mapping; understand drivers; audience-tailored comms.
- Manage expectations; resolve conflict; secure buy-in; sustain momentum.

## Client Communication

*Communicate clearly, credibly, and proactively.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Client communication** is conveying technical information **clearly and confidently**, listening well, setting expectations, and keeping clients informed — building trust throughout a project.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Translate tech→business; active listening; proactive updates.
- Hard news early + options; manage expectations; trusted-advisor relationship.

## Requirement Gathering

*Elicit, clarify, and document true requirements.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Requirement gathering** is discovering what stakeholders **truly need** — eliciting, clarifying, and documenting requirements so the solution solves the real problem, not just stated wants.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Elicit broadly; ask "why" for root need; needs vs wants.
- User stories + acceptance criteria; validate/sign-off; manage scope.

## Estimation

*Estimate Salesforce work realistically and defensibly.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Estimation** is predicting the **effort, time, and cost** of work realistically — accounting for complexity, risk, and unknowns so commitments are achievable.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Decompose; three-point/analogous/story points; include all activities.
- Risk buffer (integrations/LDV/migration); ranges + assumptions; re-estimate.

## Mentoring

*Grow others and multiply team capability.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Mentoring** is guiding less-experienced colleagues to grow their skills and careers — sharing knowledge, giving feedback, and helping them solve problems, multiplying the team's capability.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Share the "why"; guide not solve; code review as teaching; pairing.
- Growth goals + Trailhead/certs; constructive feedback; lead by example.
