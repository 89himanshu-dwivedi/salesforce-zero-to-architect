## Coding Questions

*Apex/SOQL/JS coding rounds and how to ace them.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Coding questions** in Salesforce interviews test live **Apex, SOQL, and JavaScript** — writing bulk-safe triggers, queries, and component logic, plus reasoning about governor limits.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Apex bulkification, SOQL optimization, Maps/Sets, governor limits, JS/LWC.
- Think aloud; clarify; edge cases + tests; communication is scored.

## Trigger Scenarios

*Design and reason about Apex trigger problems.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Trigger scenarios** test how you design **Apex triggers** — handling bulk data, recursion, order of execution, and choosing trigger vs declarative — for common business requirements.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- One-trigger-per-object + handler; bulkified; recursion-safe; before/after.
- Order of execution; declarative-vs-code; mixed DML/async/error edge cases.
