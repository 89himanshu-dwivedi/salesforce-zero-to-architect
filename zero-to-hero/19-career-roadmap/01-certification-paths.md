## Admin

*The foundational Salesforce Administrator certification.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

The **Salesforce Certified Administrator** is the **foundational** credential — proving you can configure and manage Salesforce: users, security, objects, automation, reports, and the core declarative platform.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Foundational: security, data model, Flow, data mgmt, analytics.
- Prereq mindset for all paths; maintained via release exams; needs hands-on.

## PD1

*Platform Developer I — Apex, LWC, and the platform.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Platform Developer I (PD1)** is the entry **developer** certification — proving you can build custom logic with **Apex**, **Lightning Web Components**, SOQL/SOSL, and the programmatic platform.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Apex/triggers/testing, SOQL/SOSL/DML, LWC basics, declarative-vs-code.
- Governor limits central; needs portfolio; gateway to PD2/JS Developer.

## JavaScript Developer

## CTA

*Certified Technical Architect — the pinnacle credential.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Certified Technical Architect (CTA)** is the **pinnacle** Salesforce certification — proving you can design and justify end-to-end enterprise solutions across data, security, integration, identity, and development, defended live before a board.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Review board: design complex scenario + present/defend to judges.
- Holistic (data/security/integration/identity/mobile/lifecycle/governance).
- Needs App+System Architect, deep experience, mock-board practice.
