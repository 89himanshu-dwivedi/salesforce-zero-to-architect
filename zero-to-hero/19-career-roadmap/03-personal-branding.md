## Resume

*Craft a results-driven Salesforce resume.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A strong **Salesforce resume** shows **impact and breadth** — not just tasks. It lists certifications, quantified achievements, and the specific clouds, tools, and technologies you've delivered.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Summary + certs high; quantified action-verb impact bullets.
- Tailor keywords for ATS; show tech breadth + concrete projects; concise.

## LinkedIn

*Build a discoverable, credible LinkedIn presence.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A strong **LinkedIn** profile makes you **discoverable to recruiters** and builds credibility — optimized headline, keyword-rich about section, certifications, and visible engagement in the Salesforce community.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Keyword headline/About; all certs + skills; recommendations.
- Active engagement + Trailblazer community; consistent presence.

## Portfolio

*Showcase real Salesforce work and projects.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- GitHub code (Apex/LWC) + READMEs; problem→result case studies.

## Trailhead

*Leverage Trailhead for skills and visible credibility.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Trailhead** is Salesforce's free learning platform. Earning **badges, superbadges, and ranks** builds real skills and a **public profile** that proves continuous learning to recruiters.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Modules/trails + Playgrounds; superbadges for applied proof.

## Certifications

*Strategically sequence and showcase certifications.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Certifications** validate your Salesforce expertise to employers. A smart **sequence** — and keeping them current — strengthens your brand and opens roles.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Admin foundation → specialize (dev/consultant/architect) → CTA.
- Sequence by prerequisite; maintain via release exams; showcase; role-aligned.
