## Multi Team Release

*Coordinate releases across many parallel teams.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Multi-team release** coordinates many teams building on **one Salesforce org** — managing parallel work, merge conflicts, environment strategy, and integration so releases ship cleanly together.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Git branching + review; modular packages; per-team + integration sandboxes.
- CI/CD test gates; release-train cadence; frequent small integration; release manager.

## CI/CD Governance

*Govern automated pipelines for quality and control.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**CI/CD governance** defines the **rules and controls** around automated build/test/deploy pipelines — quality gates, approvals, security, and standards — so automation stays fast *and* safe.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Automated gates (tests/coverage/SAST); branch protection; approval-based promotion.
- Vaulted secrets; audit trails; tested rollback; automate controls for velocity.

## Change Advisory Board

*Govern change approval and risk for production releases.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A **Change Advisory Board (CAB)** is the governance body that **reviews, approves, and schedules** production changes — assessing risk, impact, and readiness so releases are controlled and coordinated.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Assess risk/impact/rollback/conflicts; categorize standard/normal/emergency.
- Schedule around freezes; pre-approve low-risk; CI/CD-integrated; audited.
