## Complex Sharing Model

*Architect record access for intricate enterprise requirements.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A **complex sharing model** designs **record-level access** for intricate enterprise needs — combining OWD, roles, sharing rules, teams, and Apex sharing to give each user exactly the right visibility.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Restrictive OWD + hierarchy/rules/teams/territories/Apex sharing.
- Sharing sets for external; minimize rules/skew; watch recalc performance.

## Partner Community Access

*Securely grant partners scoped access via Experience Cloud.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Partner community access** gives external partners **scoped, secure** access to relevant records via Experience Cloud — using partner licenses, sharing sets, and roles so they see only their own and shared data.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Partner license; external account hierarchy + roles; sharing sets/share groups.
- Super user access; least-privilege permissions; SSO/MFA; scale-aware.

## Guest User Access

*Lock down unauthenticated public access tightly.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Guest user access** controls what **unauthenticated public visitors** can see/do on a site — and must be locked down tightly because guest users are a common **security exposure**.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Private guest model + minimal guest sharing rules; default owner for writes.
- with-sharing Apex + CRUD/FLS; minimal profile; reCAPTCHA/rate limit; monitor.

## Regulatory Compliance

*Meet GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, PCI and similar mandates.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Regulatory compliance** ensures the Salesforce solution meets legal mandates — **GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, PCI-DSS, CCPA** — for data privacy, security, auditability, and retention.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Map regs→controls; Shield encryption; Field Audit Trail + Event Monitoring.
- Least-privilege access; consent/erasure/DSAR; residency; ongoing governance.
