## Logging Framework

*Build a structured, governed logging framework in Salesforce.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

A **logging framework** captures structured records of what happens in Salesforce — errors, integrations, key events — in a consistent, queryable way for debugging, monitoring, and audit.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Storage by volume (Log object/Big Object/Platform Events); rollback-safe writes.
- Structured fields + correlation ID; configurable levels; retention; externalize.

## Splunk Integration

*Centralize Salesforce logs and events in Splunk.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Splunk integration** sends Salesforce **logs, events, and monitoring data** into Splunk — the enterprise SIEM/observability platform — for centralized search, dashboards, alerting, and security analysis.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Sources: Event Monitoring, Shield events, custom logs; add-on/HEC/streaming.
- SIEM/observability/correlation; alerts; secure transport; volume/cost control.

## Datadog Integration

*Monitor Salesforce performance and health in Datadog.*

*Beginner - plain language*

### 📏 Limits

## Observability

*Achieve full insight into system behavior and health.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Observability** is the ability to **understand a system's internal state from its outputs** — combining logs, metrics, and traces so you can detect, diagnose, and resolve issues across Salesforce and the whole stack.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Three pillars (logs/metrics/traces) + correlation IDs; vs known-only monitoring.
- Salesforce sources + Splunk/Datadog/New Relic; SLO/SLI-driven proactive alerting.
