## Org Consolidation

*Merge multiple Salesforce orgs into one.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Org consolidation** merges **multiple Salesforce orgs into one** — unifying data, metadata, and processes — to reduce cost, duplication, and complexity, often after M&A or organic sprawl.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Assess orgs; unified data/security model; cross-org dedupe + migration.
- Merge customizations; watch org limits; re-point integrations; phased cutover.

## Org Split

*Separate one org into multiple orgs.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Org split (divestiture)** separates **one Salesforce org into multiple** — extracting a business unit's data, metadata, and users into a new org, often due to a sale, spin-off, or divergent needs.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Scope unit's data/metadata/users; carve out into new org; separate shared data.
- Split/re-point integrations; validated phased cutover; compliance for divestiture.

## Global Deployment

*Roll out Salesforce across countries and regions.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Global deployment** rolls out Salesforce across **multiple countries/regions** — handling languages, currencies, time zones, local compliance, and a balance of global standards with local needs.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Single vs multi-org by residency/governance/limits; global template + local variation.
- Multi-currency/translation/locale; Hyperforce residency; CoE governance; phased rollout.

## Regional Deployment

*Deploy and govern Salesforce per region.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Regional deployment** structures Salesforce **by region** — sometimes separate regional orgs, sometimes one org partitioned by region — to meet local needs, residency, and governance while keeping global coherence.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Regional orgs vs partitioned single org (residency-driven); clean partitioning.
- Global rollup for reporting; CoE governance; regional process variations.

## Data Residency

*Keep data within required geographic boundaries.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Data residency** ensures data is **stored and processed within required geographic boundaries** — driven by laws like GDPR — using regional hosting, org strategy, and controls so regulated data stays in-country/region.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Hyperforce region selection; regional orgs if rules conflict; Shield/BYOK keys.
- Control cross-border integration/backup flows; lawful transfer; governance/audit.
