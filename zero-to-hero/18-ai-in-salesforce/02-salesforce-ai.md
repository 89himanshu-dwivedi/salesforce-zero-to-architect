## Einstein Prediction Builder

*Low-code custom predictions on any object.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Einstein Prediction Builder** lets admins build **custom predictions** (yes/no or numeric) on standard or custom objects **without code** — e.g., predict which accounts will churn — by pointing it at historical data.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Low-code binary/numeric predictions; writes score + top factors.
- Needs labeled history; guard leakage/bias; simpler than Discovery.

## Einstein Discovery

*Augmented analytics with insights and recommendations.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Einstein Discovery** (in CRM Analytics) analyzes data to explain **what happened, why, what will happen, and what to do** — surfacing statistical insights, predictions, and improvement recommendations.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Descriptive→diagnostic→predictive→prescriptive; drivers + recommendations.
- Bias checks; deploys to objects; needs CRM Analytics datasets.

## Einstein Search

*AI-powered, personalized, natural-language search.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Einstein Search** makes Salesforce search smarter — **personalized, natural-language** results with instant answers and actions, so users find records and act faster.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Personalized ranking, NL queries, actionable results; respects sharing.
- Low-setup org-wide enablement; distinct from generative answers.

## Einstein Bots

*Automated conversational chatbots for service.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Einstein Bots** are **chatbots** that handle customer conversations on chat/messaging channels — answering FAQs, collecting info, and automating tasks before (or instead of) reaching a human agent.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Dialogs + intents (NLU) + entities + actions; Omni-Channel/Messaging.
- Deflection + context handoff; evolving toward Agentforce (generative).
