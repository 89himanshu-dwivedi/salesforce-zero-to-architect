## Bring Your Own Model

*Use external or custom LLMs behind the Trust Layer.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Bring Your Own Model (BYOM)** lets you connect **external or custom LLMs** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, your own hosted/fine-tuned model) to Salesforce via **Model Builder**, used by Prompt Builder/Agentforce behind the Trust Layer.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Register partner/own-provider/custom models; exposed via Models API.
- Trust Layer governs all; per-use-case choice; provider abstraction.

## OpenAI Integration

*Use OpenAI GPT models in Salesforce.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**OpenAI integration** connects **GPT models** (e.g., GPT-4 class) to Salesforce — as the default Einstein-hosted option or via your own **Azure OpenAI** account — for generative features behind the Trust Layer.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Einstein-managed vs Azure OpenAI BYOM; strong general reasoning.
- Trust Layer zero-retention; right-size tier; residency via Azure; Models API.

## Anthropic Integration

*Use Anthropic Claude models in Salesforce.*

### 🌱 Simple

*Beginner - plain language*

**Anthropic integration** connects **Claude** models to Salesforce — via Einstein or your own **Amazon Bedrock** account — for generative features, known for strong reasoning, long context, and safety focus, behind the Trust Layer.

### 📏 Limits

*Governor & platform limits*

- Einstein-managed or Bedrock BYOM; long context, strong reasoning, safety.
