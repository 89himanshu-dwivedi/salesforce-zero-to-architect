# 2.6 Parallel Processing Ki Taakat Aur Uske Chhupe Khatre

Parallel processing hi Bulk API ki **asli strength** hai.

Lekin:

> **Theory aur practice me bada farak ho sakta hai.**

Aap theoretically multiple batches ek saath chalane ka plan bana sakte ho, lekin production me:

- batches ek saath pick na hon,
- locks ke liye competition ho,
- records fail hon,
- retries hon,
- effective Degree of Parallelism expected se kam ho,
- aur throughput gir jaye.

Isliye:

```text
Parallel Processing
        ↓
High Throughput Potential
        ↓
BUT
        ↓
Poor Architecture?
        ↓
Lock Contention + Failures + Retries
        ↓
Throughput Down
        ↓
Parallel load can perform worse than expected
```

Salesforce current guidance bhi parallel mode ko faster loading ke liye default/preferred starting point batati hai, lekin record lock contention ke risk aur failed-record resubmission ko explicitly highlight karti hai. citeturn0search0turn0search3

# Parallel Run Me Kya-Kya Galat Ho Sakta Hai

## “Theory me 10 batches, practice me kuch aur.”

Aapne soch liya:

```text
10 batches
   ↓
Sab parallel chalenge
   ↓
Time aadha ho jayega
```

Lekin real environment me aisa **guaranteed** nahi hai.

---

## ❌ Saare Batches Parallel Nahi Chalte

Sab batches ek saath pick/process nahi hote.

Actual processing:

- available capacity
- queueing
- platform load
- other asynchronous work
- batch/job design

par depend kar sakti hai.

Simple model:

```text
10 Batches
   ↓
Available capacity
   ↓
Kuch batches process
   ↓
Next batches wait
   ↓
Actual parallelism
```

Salesforce uses a queue-based framework for asynchronous processing, and requests can be delayed when an org has a large number of unprocessed requests in the queue. citeturn0search0
