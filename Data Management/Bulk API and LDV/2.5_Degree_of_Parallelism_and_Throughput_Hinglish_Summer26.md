## 2.5 Degree Of Parallelism Aur Throughput — Do Maapne Wale Numbers

Parallel processing ko samajhne aur measure karne ke liye do important concepts hain:

1. **Degree of Parallelism (DoP)** — kitna kaam ek saath effectively ho raha hai.
2. **Throughput** — per second kitne records process ho rahe hain.

Simple language me:

```text
Degree of Parallelism
=
Kitna kaam simultaneously execute hua
```

aur:

```text
Throughput
=
Kitne records / unit time process hue
```

Dono related hain, lekin **same cheez nahi hain**.

Salesforce ke historical Bulk API guidance me bhi degree of parallelism ko throughput se directly related performance concept ke roop me explain kiya gaya hai. Saath hi Salesforce ye bhi clearly explain karta hai ki DoP badhne se throughput necessarily same proportion me nahi badhta. citeturn0search9

# Degree Of Parallelism — Formula Aur Ek Saaf Example

## Source Formula

**Kaam ka total time ÷ actual liya gaya time.**

```text
Degree of Parallelism
=
Total work duration
/
Actual elapsed time
```

Isko easy words me:

> Agar saare batches one-by-one chalte, to total kitna time lagta?

versus

> Actual parallel run me job ko kitna elapsed time laga?

### Formula

```text
DoP = Total work duration / Actual elapsed time
```
