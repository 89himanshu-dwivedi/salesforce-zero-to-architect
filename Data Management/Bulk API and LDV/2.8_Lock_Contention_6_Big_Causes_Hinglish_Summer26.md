# Chhah Causes Ek Nazar Me

Sirf parent-child relationship hi nahi — multiple mechanisms lock contention create ya worsen kar sakte hain.

Source ke 6 causes:

1. **Referential Integrity — Master-Detail / Lookup**
2. **Roll-Up Summary Fields**
3. **Triggers**
4. **Workflows / Process Flows**
5. **Group Membership**
6. **Overlapping Runs**

> **Exam point:** Source ke according **Group Membership** wala cause exam me sabse zyada aata hai.

---

## 1. Referential Integrity — MD / Lookup

Related data update karte waqt parent lock ho sakta hai.

Source:

> **Master-detail aur kuch lookup types me.**

Simple:

```text
Parent
   ↓
Related Child
   ↓
Parent / related resource lock
   ↓
Another parallel batch wants same resource
   ↓
Lock Contention
```

Ye wahi pattern hai jo Account–Order example me dekha:

```text
Batch 1 → Account 1
Batch 2 → Account 1
          ↓
     Lock Competition
```

---

## 2. Roll-Up Summary Fields

Agar master record par **Roll-Up Summary Field** hai aur aap child records update kar rahe ho:

```text
Child Record Update
       ↓
Roll-Up Calculation
       ↓
Master Record affected
       ↓
Master lock
       ↓
Multiple batches?
       ↓
Lock Contention
```

Source ka exact idea:

> **Master record par roll-up hai aur aap child records update kar rahe ho — Salesforce master ko lock karega. Agar child records kai batches me faile hain to contention pakka.**

### Simple Example

```text
Account
   ↓
Roll-Up Summary
   ↓
Orders / Child Records
```

Agar:

```text
Batch 1 → Account 1 ke child records
Batch 2 → Account 1 ke child records
```

to roll-up calculation ki wajah se same master par competition ho sakta hai.

### Architect View

Roll-up ko load architecture me ignore mat karo.

```text
Child Volume
+
Roll-Up Fields
+
Parallel Batches
=
Potential Lock Contention
```

---

## 3. Triggers

Load ke waqt triggers fire ho rahe hain to:

> **Wo bhi records lock kar sakte hain.**

Flow:

```text
Bulk Load
   ↓
Trigger Fires
   ↓
Trigger Updates Related Records
   ↓
Additional Locks
   ↓
Parallel Batch
   ↓
Possible Contention
```
