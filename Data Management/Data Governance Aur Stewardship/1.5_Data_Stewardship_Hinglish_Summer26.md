# 1.5 Data Stewardship — Data Ki Rakhwali Kaun Karta Hai

## Steward ka kaam, tools aur human touch

Governance ke rules **bina stewardship ke bekaar hain**.

**Data Steward** wo responsible person hota hai jo data collection ko monitor karta hai, users ke data-related sawaalon ka jawab deta hai, aur systems ke beech data conflicts ko resolve karne mein help karta hai.

---

## 1. Steward Ka Roz Ka Kaam — Monitor, Answer, Fix

### Admin days wali kahani

Admin days mein agar speaker ko koi address pasand nahi aata tha, to wo khud baith kar address change kar deti thi ya users ko jaakar bolti thi ki data theek karo.

Iska main point:

> Stewardship sirf rules banana nahi hai; kisi responsible person ko data quality ko continuously dekhna aur improve karna hota hai.

### Monitor Karna

- Data collection monitor karna
- Data quality observe karna
- Galat/incomplete data identify karna
- Users ko correction ke liye guide karna
- Standards consistently follow ho rahe hain ya nahi dekhna

### Sawaalon Ka Jawaab Dena

Ye steward ki **key activities** mein se ek hai.

Users sahi information enter karna chahte hain, lekin multiple options hone par confusion hota hai:

> "Kaunsa select karun?"

Steward clear guidance deta hai.

Examples:
- Customer type kaunsa choose karein?
- Address format kya hona chahiye?
- Kaunsa Account record correct hai?
- Do similar records mein se kaunsa use karein?
- Field ka standard format kya hai?

### Conflicts Resolve Karna

Agar do systems mein same customer ka address different hai, to conflict resolve karna padega.

Example:

```text
System A → 123 Main Street
System B → 123 Main St.
```

Steward ko determine karna hoga:
- Correct value kya hai?
- Kaunsa system authoritative hai?
- Kis source ko follow karna hai?
- Dusre system ko update karna hai ya nahi?

Customer-facing teams ke paas kai baar correct business context hota hai, isliye stewardship mein business users ka input important hai.

### Consistency Ensure Karna

Goal hai ki systems ke beech **consistent truth** maintain rahe.

```text
Users
  ↓
Data Entry
  ↓
Steward Review / Guidance
  ↓
Correct & Consistent Data
  ↓
Multiple Systems
```

Steward ka role har system ka owner hona zaroori nahi; important role data standard aur business meaning ko consistently maintain karwana hai.
