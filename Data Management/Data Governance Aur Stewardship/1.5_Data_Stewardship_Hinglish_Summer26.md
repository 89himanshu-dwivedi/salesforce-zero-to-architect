# 1.5 Data Stewardship — Data Ki Rakhwali Kaun Karta Hai

## Steward ka kaam, tools aur human touch

Governance ke rules **bina stewardship ke bekaar hain**.

**Data Steward** wo responsible person hota hai jo data collection ko monitor karta hai, users ke data-related sawaalon ka jawab deta hai, aur systems ke beech data conflicts ko resolve karne mein help karta hai.

---

## 1. Steward Ka Roz Ka Kaam — Monitor, Answer, Fix

### Admin days wali kahani

Admin days mein agar speaker ko koi address pasand nahi aata tha, to wo khud baith kar address change kar deti thi ya users ko jaakar bolti thi ki data theek karo.

Iska main point:

> Stewardship sirf rules banana nahi hai; kisi responsible person ko data quality ko continuously dekhna aur improve karna hota hai.

### Monitor Karna

- Data collection monitor karna
- Data quality observe karna
- Galat/incomplete data identify karna
- Users ko correction ke liye guide karna
- Standards consistently follow ho rahe hain ya nahi dekhna

### Sawaalon Ka Jawaab Dena

Ye steward ki **key activities** mein se ek hai.

Users sahi information enter karna chahte hain, lekin multiple options hone par confusion hota hai:

> "Kaunsa select karun?"

Steward clear guidance deta hai.

Examples:
- Customer type kaunsa choose karein?
- Address format kya hona chahiye?
- Kaunsa Account record correct hai?
- Do similar records mein se kaunsa use karein?
- Field ka standard format kya hai?

### Conflicts Resolve Karna

Agar do systems mein same customer ka address different hai, to conflict resolve karna padega.

Example:

```text
System A → 123 Main Street
System B → 123 Main St.
```

Steward ko determine karna hoga:
- Correct value kya hai?
- Kaunsa system authoritative hai?
- Kis source ko follow karna hai?
- Dusre system ko update karna hai ya nahi?

Customer-facing teams ke paas kai baar correct business context hota hai, isliye stewardship mein business users ka input important hai.

### Consistency Ensure Karna

Goal hai ki systems ke beech **consistent truth** maintain rahe.

```text
Users
  ↓
Data Entry
  ↓
Steward Review / Guidance
  ↓
Correct & Consistent Data
  ↓
Multiple Systems
```

Steward ka role har system ka owner hona zaroori nahi; important role data standard aur business meaning ko consistently maintain karwana hai.

## 3. Steward Ke Tools — Scoring, Dashboards Aur Analyzers

Main idea:

> **Package kharido ya khud formula/automation se banao.**

### Data Quality Scoring

Ready-made packages data-quality score calculate karne mein help kar sakte hain.

Package use nahi karna ho to business rules ke according Salesforce capabilities se scoring model design kiya ja sakta hai.

Example:

```text
Completeness  = 40%
Accuracy      = 30%
Consistency   = 20%
Freshness     = 10%

Overall Data Quality Score
```

Exact formula business requirement par depend karega.

### Score Se Process Block

Data-quality score ko process gate ki tarah use kiya ja sakta hai.

```text
Sales Opportunity
      ↓
Required information complete?
      ↓
   Yes → Continue
   No  → Fix Data
```

Lekin har situation mein complete process block karna zaroori nahi. Critical data ke liye blocking, non-critical data ke liye warning/exception better ho sakta hai.

### Data Quality Dashboards

Dashboard mein visible ho sakta hai:
- Missing fields
- Invalid values
- Duplicate records
- Data-quality score
- Quality gaps
- Trends
- Team/business-unit issues

Script ka important point:

> Dashboard ko share karte waqt tone **positive** hona chahiye — blame game nahi.

### Sales Ops / PMO Support

Sales Operations ya PMO dashboards ko monitor karke:
- Managers se support maang sakte hain
- Training identify kar sakte hain
- Process problems identify kar sakte hain
- Data-quality initiatives drive kar sakte hain

### AppExchange Analyzers

Data-analysis tools completeness aur usage assess karne mein help kar sakte hain.

Ye samajhne mein help kar sakte hain:
- Kaunsa field useful hai?
- Kaunsa rarely use hota hai?
- Kaunsa field retire karna chahiye?
- Kaunsa field business-critical hai?

Analyzer recommendation deta hai; final retirement decision business + architecture + dependency analysis par hona chahiye.

## 4. Regulatory Reporting

Agar Salesforce ka data regulator ko report hota hai, to data-quality monitoring aur bhi critical ho jaati hai.

```text
Salesforce Data
      ↓
Data Quality Checks
      ↓
Validation / Reconciliation
      ↓
Regulatory Report
      ↓
Regulator
```

Incorrect, incomplete ya inconsistent data ka impact normal CRM reporting se zyada serious ho sakta hai.

## 5. Data Quality Dashboard Ka Asar

### Success Path

```text
Quality Dashboard Banaya
        ↓
Scores + Gaps Visible
        ↓
Team Communication
        ↓
Positive Framing
        ↓
Managers Support Karte Hain
        ↓
Team Ko Time / Guidance Milti Hai
        ↓
Data Clean Aur Reportable
```

Positive framing:

> "Chalo isko better karte hain."

Focus problem solve karne par hota hai, person ko blame karne par nahi.

### Failure Path

```text
Dashboard Banaya
      ↓
Blame Framing
      ↓
"Dekho kisne kharab data daala"
      ↓
Defensive Behaviour
      ↓
Users Dashboard Ignore Karte Hain
      ↓
Dashboard Dead
```

Objective **people ko punish karna nahi, data ko improve karna** hai.

## 6. Field Retirement Insight

Analyzers sirf quality problems identify karne ke liye nahi hote.

### Field A
- Rarely used
- Low business value
- Duplicate information
- High maintenance cost

→ Retirement candidate ho sakta hai.

### Field B
- Frequently used
- Important reporting dependency
- Integration dependency
- Regulatory/business significance

→ Change request par extra caution chahiye.

**Field retire karne se pehle** reports, flows, integrations, Apex, formulas, validation rules, external systems aur downstream dependencies check karne chahiye.

## 7. Sabse Underrated Tool — Hafte Mein Ek Ghanta "Aake Poochho"

### Conversation bhi ek governance tool hai

Ek important tool aksar miss ho jata hai:

> **Conversation**

Conversation ho sakti hai:
- In-person
- Phone par
- Conference call
- Office hour
- Team discussion

### Weekly Office Hour Ka Effect

```text
Admin Ne 1 Hour Block Kiya
        ↓
Hafte Mein Ek Baar
        ↓
Safe Space Bana
        ↓
Users Questions Poochte Hain
        ↓
"Best Way Kya Hai?"
        ↓
Steward Guidance Deta Hai
        ↓
Users Correct Data Enter Karte Hain
        ↓
```

Manager ka pressure bhi kam ho sakta hai kyunki users ko trusted person se direct clarification mil jaati hai.

### Kyu Kaam Karta Hai

> Log galat data hamesha laparwahi ki wajah se nahi daalte.

Kai baar reason simple hota hai:

**"Mujhe pata hi nahi tha ki sahi kya hai."**

Ek safe jagah dena hi aadha problem solve kar sakta hai.

## 8. Normal / Success / Failure / Watch-out

| Situation | Success Path | Failure / Error Path | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data monitoring | Regular monitoring | Issues unnoticed | Ownership unclear |
| User question | Steward gives clear answer | User guesses | Business terminology unclear |
| System conflict | Source/owner identified | Systems keep different values | No source of truth |
| Quality score | Used for improvement | Used only for blaming | Score must be meaningful |
| Dashboard | Actionable and reviewed | Dashboard ignored | Dashboard needs owner |
| Chatter | Collaboration and traceability | No adoption / disabled | Summer ’26 new orgs have Chatter disabled by default |
| Field retirement | Dependency analysis first | Dependencies break | Check reports, automation, integrations |
| Office hour | Safe questions + guidance | Users hesitate | Avoid blame culture |

## 10. Pro vs Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Clear ownership of data quality | Stewardship needs dedicated time |
| Users get a person to ask | Can become dependent on one person |
| Faster resolution of data questions | Manual intervention can increase |
| Helps resolve system conflicts | Conflicts may require multiple teams |
| Dashboards make quality visible | Bad metrics can create wrong behaviour |
| Positive collaboration improves adoption | Blame culture can kill adoption |
| Office hours solve confusion early | Requires regular participation |
| Analyzers help identify unused/important fields | Recommendations need human/business validation |
| Supports regulatory data quality | Higher governance effort and accountability |

# 11. Interview Questions — Junior

### Q1. What is Data Stewardship?

**Answer:**  
Data Stewardship means assigning responsible people to monitor data quality, answer data-related questions, guide users and help resolve data conflicts.

### Q2. Difference between Data Governance and Data Stewardship?

**Answer:**
- **Governance** → rules, policies, standards and decision framework.
- **Stewardship** → those rules ko day-to-day business mein apply karna.

```text
Governance = What should happen?
Stewardship = Who helps make it happen?
```

### Q3. What does a Data Steward do?

**Answer:**
- Monitor data
- Answer user questions
- Resolve data issues
- Help resolve cross-system conflicts
- Maintain consistency
- Support quality improvement

### Q4. Why are dashboards useful?

**Answer:**  
They make quality gaps visible and help teams identify where corrective action is required.

# 12. Interview Questions — Mid-Level

### Q5. How would you handle different customer addresses in two systems?

**Answer:**
1. Identify both values.
2. Understand business ownership.
3. Identify authoritative source.
4. Confirm correct value with the responsible business team.
5. Correct the appropriate system.
6. Prevent recurrence through process/rules/integration controls.

### Q6. Should a data-quality score always block the business process?

**Answer:**  
No. Blocking should depend on business criticality.

```text
Critical data   → Block
Non-critical    → Warning / Exception / Follow-up
```

### Q7. How do you make a data-quality dashboard effective?

**Answer:**
- Meaningful metrics
- Actionable gaps
- Assigned owner
- Trend tracking
- Regular review
- No blame-based reporting

### Q8. What is the steward's role when users are confused?

**Answer:**  
The steward provides the approved business interpretation and guides users toward the correct value.

# 13. Interview Questions — Senior

### Q9. How would you design stewardship for multiple Salesforce orgs?

**Answer:**
Define:
- Data domains
- Data owners
- Data stewards
- System ownership
- Data standards
- Escalation process
- Source-of-truth rules
- Quality metrics
- Cross-system reconciliation

### Q10. Salesforce and an external system both claim to be the source of truth. What do you do?

**Answer:**  
Do not automatically choose Salesforce. Determine:
- Data domain
- Attribute
- Business process
- Business owner
- Where data is created
- Where it is mastered
- Who consumes it
- Conflict-resolution rule

### Q11. How would you prevent a dashboard from becoming useless?

**Answer:**

```text
Metric
 ↓
Owner
 ↓
Threshold
 ↓
Action
 ↓
Review
```

A dashboard without ownership and action is only reporting, not stewardship.

# 15. Interview Questions — Architect

### Q15. How would you design enterprise Data Stewardship?

**Answer:**

Start with **data domains**, not Salesforce objects.

```text
Customer Domain
     ↓
Data Owner
     ↓
Data Steward
     ↓
Systems of Record
     ↓
Data Standards
     ↓
Quality Rules
     ↓
Monitoring
     ↓
Issue / Conflict Resolution
```

Then map those responsibilities to Salesforce, Data 360 and external systems.

### Q16. Business says "Salesforce must be the source of truth for everything." What do you do?

**Answer:**

Challenge the statement at architecture level.

A single platform does not automatically mean a single source of truth.

Ask:
- Which data domain?
- Which attribute?
- Which business process?
- Who owns it?
- Where is it created?
- Where is it mastered?
- Who consumes it?

The result may be **domain-specific systems of record**.

### Q17. How would you architect data-quality governance across Salesforce and external systems?

**Answer:**

```text
             Data Governance
                    ↓
        ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        ↓                       ↓
   Data Ownership          Data Standards
        ↓                       ↓
   Data Stewardship       Quality Rules
        └───────────┬───────────┘
                    ↓
             Systems of Record
                    ↓
             Integrations
                    ↓
          Quality Monitoring
                    ↓
          Issue / Conflict Mgmt
```

The Architect should ensure governance exists **across the ecosystem**, not only inside Salesforce.

### Q18. How does Data 360 affect stewardship?

## Core Line

> **Governance defines the rules; stewardship makes those rules work in day-to-day business.**

And the main human lesson:

> **People often enter bad data because they don't know what the correct data is. Giving them a safe place to ask can solve the problem before a technical control is required.**
