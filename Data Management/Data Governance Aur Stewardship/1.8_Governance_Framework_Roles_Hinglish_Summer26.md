# 1.8 Governance Framework Ke Roles, Committee Aur Council

**Steward, Ops, Committee, Council, Office**

Ek governance framework mein kai layers hote hain.

Basic flow:

```text
End Users / Primary Stewards
          ↓
Ops / Admins / Super Users
          ↓
Governance Committee
          ↓
Data Governance Council
          ↓
Steering Committee
          ↓
Data Governance Office
```

Har layer ka apna role hota hai.

---

# 1. Primary Steward = End User

## Salesforce Me Chhupna Nahi Hota

> **Jo data banata hai, wahi uska custodian hai.**

Sales, Support, Marketing ke log jo daily business karte hain aur phone/mobile se data enter karte hain, wo effectively **data stewards** hain, kyunki wo data ke custodian aur owner hote hain.

## Salesforce Ka Sach

Salesforce mein **"mujhe nahi pata kisne daala"** wali situation avoid ki ja sakti hai because record ownership, audit information aur user context se data activity ko trace karne ke mechanisms available hote hain.

Main governance idea:

> Data create karne wale user ki data-quality responsibility important hai.

---

## 💼 Sales Users

Sales users:

- Opportunity data
- Account data

ke primary business owners/custodians ho sakte hain.

---

## 🎧 Support Users

Support users:

- Case data
- Customer interaction data

ke primary business custodians ho sakte hain.

---

## 📧 Marketing Users

Marketing users:

- Lead data
- Campaign data

ke primary business custodians ho sakte hain.

---

## 📱 Field / Mobile Users

Field/mobile users phone se data enter karte hain.

Isliye:

> **Data-entry quality yahin banti bhi hai aur bigad bhi sakti hai.**

Agar field user ko correct value pata hai aur correct process follow karta hai, to downstream data quality improve hoti hai.
