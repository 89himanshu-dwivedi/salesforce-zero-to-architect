### Simple Definition

**Data Governance** ka matlab sirf "data ko secure rakhna" nahi hai.

Governance ka broader question hai:

> **Kaunsa data collect hoga, kis purpose ke liye hoga, kaun access karega, kaun responsible hoga, quality kaise maintain hogi, aur problem hone par decision/remediation kaise hoga?**

Salesforce ke current learning material mein governance ko policies, standards, processes aur accountability ke framework ke roop mein describe kiya gaya hai jo data ko trusted, secure, usable aur compliant rakhne mein help karta hai.

**Data Stewardship** governance ko day-to-day operational level par execute karne ka practice hai: quality monitor karna, issues investigate karna, remediation coordinate karna aur business expectations ke saath data ko aligned rakhna.

## Shuruaat — 18 Mahine Administrator

Career ki shuruaat admin role se hui.

Iska governance se important connection hai:

- Sharing aur visibility daily responsibilities ka part rahe.
- Data access ke practical problems directly dekhe.
- Sirf configuration nahi, balki **who can see what** ka business impact samjha.
- Aage architect-level governance understanding build karne ka foundation bana.

### Architect Perspective

Admin experience governance ke liye useful hai kyunki architecture ultimately configuration ko business responsibility se connect karta hai.

Example:

```text
Business says:
"Sales team ko customer data chahiye."

Architect asks:
- Kaunsi team?
- Kaunsa customer data?
- Kis geography ka?
- Read ya Edit?
- Sensitive fields ka kya?
- External users ka kya?
- Audit requirement?
- Retention?
```
