# 1.2 Data Governance Ke 6 Core Principles

Data Governance basically **6 sawaalon ka jawaab** hai:

1. Kya collect ho raha hai?
2. Secure kaise rakhoge aur kyu?
3. Kaun create/manage/update karega?
4. Quality ke standards kya honge?
5. Data kitna available aur usable hai?
6. Conflict aane par process kya hoga?

### Simple Mental Model

```text
WHAT
 ↓
WHY
 ↓
WHO
 ↓
HOW GOOD
 ↓
HOW USABLE
 ↓
WHAT IF CONFLICT
```

# Governance Ke 6 Sawaal — Poori Definition Ek Frame Me

## 1️⃣ Kya Collect Ho Raha Hai — WHAT

> **Kaunsa data collect/track karna hai?**

Har field ka business purpose hona chahiye.

### Architect Questions

- Ye field kyun hai?
- Kaun use karta hai?
- Kaunse process mein use hoti hai?
- Reporting mein use hoti hai?
- Integration ko chahiye?
- AI/Agent ko chahiye?
- Legal/compliance reason hai?
- Agar field remove kar dein to kya break hoga?

### Anti-Pattern

```text
"We may need it someday."
        ↓
Create field
        ↓
Nobody owns it
        ↓
Nobody maintains it
        ↓
Data becomes stale
```

### Better

```text
Business Purpose
      ↓
Data Requirement
      ↓
Field/Object
      ↓
Owner
      ↓
Quality Rule
      ↓
Lifecycle
```

---

# 2️⃣ Secure Kaise Rakhoge Aur Kyu — WHY

Security ka question sirf:

> "Who can see it?"

tak limited nahi hona chahiye.

Question:

> **Data ko protect kyun karna hai aur kis risk ke against?**

Examples:

- PII
- Financial information
- Health-related information
- Customer confidential information
- Internal business data
- Regulatory information

GDPR jaise regulations ke context mein data collection aur processing ka **purpose** bhi important governance conversation hai.

### Architect Questions

```text
What data?
     ↓
Why collected?
     ↓
Who needs it?
     ↓
What risk?
     ↓
What protection?
     ↓
How long?
     ↓
How monitored?
```

### Important Distinction

```text
Security
≠
Complete Governance
```

Security governance ka ek major component hai, lekin ownership, quality, availability, lifecycle aur conflict resolution bhi governance ka part hain.

---

# 3️⃣ Kaun Create / Manage / Update Karega — WHO

Ye **ownership + responsibility** ka question hai.

Data ke liye clearly define hona chahiye:

- Kaun create karega?
- Kaun update karega?
- Kaun approve karega?
- Kaun maintain karega?
- Kaun quality issue resolve karega?
- Kaun final business decision lega?

### Example

```text
Data Owner
    ↓
Customer Business Team

Data Steward
    ↓
Monitors quality + coordinates correction

Technical Team
    ↓
Implements validation/integration/security

Users
    ↓
Create/update according to process
```

> **Technical team data ko implement kar sakti hai; business owner data ka meaning aur accountability own karta hai.**
