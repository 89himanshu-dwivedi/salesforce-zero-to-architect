# 1. Ye Blog Post Kya Hai Aur Kyu Padhni Chahiye

> **Sirf governance nahi — MDM aur LDV bhi cover hote hain.**

Blog ka naam:

**Salesforce Anti-Patterns — A Cautionary Tale**

Ye classic Salesforce style mein ek **story** ke through implementation dikhata hai jahan bahut bade data volumes load kiye jaate hain.

Story ka main lesson ye hai ki ek implementation decision isolated nahi hota.

```text
Decision
   ↓
Immediate Result
   ↓
Knock-On Effect
   ↓
Another Problem
   ↓
Performance / Operations / User Impact
```

## 📖 Story Format

Ek implementation ki kahani hai:

- Decisions liye jaate hain
- Decisions ke consequences aate hain
- Ek problem doosri problem create karti hai
- Team ko baad mein root cause samajhna padta hai

Salesforce ka original article specifically implementation anti-patterns ko story ke form mein explain karta hai. citeturn0search0turn0search1

## 📋 Governance + Strategy

Governance part cover hota hai:

- User provisioning

- Feature governance
- Record ownership
- Sharing strategy
- Business-process design

## 🌟 Master Data Management

MDM perspective se story important hai because data ko sirf Salesforce records ke roop mein nahi dekhna hai.

Questions:

- Data ka source kya hai?
- Data ka ownership kya hai?
- Data ko kaise maintain karna hai?
- Different systems ke beech consistency kaise maintain hogi?

Yaani implementation ko **enterprise data perspective** se dekhna hai.
