# 1.7 Data Quality Ko Affect Karne Wale Factors

**Age, Completeness, Accuracy, Consistency, Duplicates, Usage**

Exam mein poochha jaata hai ki **good quality data assess kaise karein**. Ye 6 factors iska core answer hain.

Saath mein data ka **linear lifecycle** bhi samjho — data create hone se lekar archive / fall out of use tak.

---

# 1. 6 Factors Jo Data Quality Decide Karte Hain

> **Ye exam mein directly aate hain.**

## ⌛ 1. Age

Data kitna purana hai aur kya wo **up-to-date** hai.

Migration ya naye business unit ko onboard karte waqt ye pehla sawaal hota hai:

> Data kahan se aayega aur kitna purana hai?

Example:

```text
Customer Data
Created: 2018
Last Updated: 2020
Current Year: 2026
```

Aise data ko blindly migrate karna quality risk create kar sakta hai.

---

## 🧩 2. Completeness

Kitne fields actually bhare ja rahe hain.

Example:

```text
100 Customer Records

Email populated       → 98
Phone populated       → 72
Industry populated    → 45
Address populated     → 91
```

Completeness se design decisions nikal sakte hain:

> Kya kisi point par field required banani chahiye?

Agar important field consistently blank hai, to organization ko decide karna pad sakta hai ki:
- Field required honi chahiye
- Conditional required honi chahiye
- Ya field actually useful hi nahi hai

---

## 🎯 3. Accuracy

Data actually correct hai ya nahi.

Examples:

- Phone number correct hai?
- Address correct hai?
- Customer information current/correct hai?

Source script mein **Data.com, D&B, Experian** jaise tools ko cross-check ke example ke roop mein diya gaya hai.

### Current Salesforce note

**Data.com ko current Salesforce solution ke roop mein treat nahi karna chahiye.** Salesforce ke official retirement documentation ke according Data.com officially retired hai. Data.com license expiration se Clean button, Data.com tab, Get Contacts aur other Data.com functionality affect hoti hai; **Duplicate Management itself is not affected by Data.com license expiration.** citeturn0search6turn0search16

Isliye script ka Data.com reference **historical example** ke roop mein samjho.

---

## 🔄 4. Consistency

Har system mein same customer ke liye consistent information honi chahiye.

Example:

```text
Salesforce
Phone → 9999999999

ERP
Phone → 8888888888
```

Ye consistency problem hai.

Script ka principle:

> Same customer ka same phone, same date of birth.

Aur governance perspective se ideally unnecessary duplicate representations nahi hone chahiye.

---

## 📋 5. Duplicates

Bahut zyada duplicates nahi hone chahiye.

Example:

```text
ABC Company
ABC Corporation
ABC Corp.
```

Agar ye same organization hain aur separate records ban gaye hain, to reporting, ownership aur customer view impact ho sakta hai.

Script ka important point:

> **Duplicates zyada honge to reports trustworthy nahi rahengi.**

### Current Salesforce

Salesforce currently provides **Matching Rules + Duplicate Rules** for duplicate detection and handling. Matching Rules define how records are identified as duplicates, while Duplicate Rules define what happens when a duplicate is found. Salesforce also provides duplicate jobs, duplicate record sets and the Potential Duplicates component. citeturn0search0turn0search4
