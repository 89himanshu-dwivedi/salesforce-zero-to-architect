## 4.6Pillar 2 — DSAR Process Aur Security Ko Aasaan Banao"Mera saara data dikhao" ka jawaab+

Doosra pillar: subject access requests ka jawaab dena aasaan banao, aur data access ko review karke minimum necessary tak restrict karo.

### Data Subject Access Request — Sabse Mushkil Sawaal Ka Jawaab

**Automated hona zaroori nahi, likha hua hona zaroori hai.**

Koi aake kehta hai: *"Mujhe wo saara data batao jo aapke paas mera hai, aur wo kaise process ho raha hai."* Ye GDPR aur baaki regulations ke sabse complex requests me se ek hai.

**DSAR Aane Par Kya Hoga**

**DSAR AayiCustomer ne apna data maanga**

**Aapke Paas Process Hai?**

**Success**

**Fully AutomatedButton dabao, saari PII collect ho jaye**

**Ya Sirf Likhe Hue Steps"Ye steps follow karo" — ye bhi kaafi hai**

**Scratch Se Nahi Karna PadaTime par response**

**Failure**

**Koi Process NahiHar baar zero se shuru**

**Har System Manually DhoondoKaun jaanta hai kahan kya hai**

**Deadline MissRegulatory exposure**

**Normal stepSuccess pathFailure / error pathWatch-out**

**Realistic Expectation**

Ideal hota ki ek button daba kar sab kuch mil jaye. Par agar aisa nahi hai, to bhi theek hai — bas **steps likhe hue hon** taaki har baar scratch se na karna pade.

### Security Review — Least Privilege Aur Just-In-Time Access

**Order management system ka example.**

Ensure karo ki **sirf unhi logon ke paas access ho jinhe chahiye**, aur unke paas bhi **sirf us waqt** ho jab chahiye.

**Order Management System Ka Access Design**

**Order System Me Purchase HistoryCustomer ne ab tak kya-kya khareeda**

**Support Agent Ko Access ChahiyeKya sach me?**

**Kab Aur Kitna Access?**

**Success**

**Sirf Call Ke DauraanUs specific customer ka**

**Just-In-Time AccessScenario-based restriction**

**Exposure Bahut KamBreach ho bhi to nuksaan limited**

**Failure**

**Hamesha Poora AccessHar customer ka har order**

**Bahut Bada Exposure SurfaceEk compromised account = sab data**

**Compliance Risk"Minimum necessary" fail**

**Normal stepSuccess pathFailure / error pathWatch-out**

**Sochne Ka Tarika**

Scenarios ke through socho: **kis waqt** kis insaan ko **kaunsa** data chahiye. Phir access ko un entities tak restrict karo. Blanket access sabse aasaan aur sabse khatarnak option hai.
