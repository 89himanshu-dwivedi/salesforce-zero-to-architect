## 4.1 Privacy Regulations Ka Naya Daur — GDPR Sirf Shuruaat Thi
**Kyu har naye law par panic nahi karna**

GDPR ke baad lagaataar naye privacy regulations aa rahe hain. Customers architects se poochhte hain "kya hum compliant hain?" — is section me samjho ki har regulation par naya project kyu galat approach hai.

### Ye Privacy Group Kyu Bana — GDPR Wala Sea Change

**GDPR se pehle bhi privacy laws the, par ye alag tha.**

**GDPR Se Pehle**

Privacy directives pehle bhi the — par unka scale aur seriousness alag thi.

**GDPR Aaya**

Europe me privacy ke saath deal karne ka tareeka hi badal gaya — ek **sea change**.

**Poori Duniya Par Asar**

Globalised economy aur consumers ke duniya bhar me move karne ki wajah se har doosre desh par asar pada.

**Bahut Investment Hui**

Salesforce aur uske customers dono ne GDPR par bahut time aur paisa lagaya.

**Phir Realisation**

"Ye khatam nahi hua — ye to **series ka pehla** regulation tha."

**Aaj Ki Reality**

Ab hum ek aisi duniya me hain jahan **privacy regulations ek norm** ban chuke hain. Kuch GDPR jaise dikhte hain, kuch usse zyada prescriptive hain, kuch kam.

### Client Ka Sawaal — "Kya Hum Compliant Hain?"

**Har naye regulation par yahi panic dohraata hai.**

Customers architects ke paas aate hain: *"Kya hum GDPR compliant hain?"* Phir California ka CCPA aaya: *"Kya hum CCPA compliant hain? Wo hai kya? Kya har naye regulation par ek major privacy push karna padega?"*

### Har Regulation Par Naya Project?

**Naya Regulation Aaya — CCPA, LGPD, ya koi aur**

**Aapka Approach Kya Hai?**

**Success**

- **Holistic Privacy Framework** — Common themes par based
- **Sirf Delta Ka Kaam** — Naye law ka farak handle karo
- **Sustainable** — Har naye law par panic nahi

**Failure**

- **Har Baar Naya Project** — Regulation-by-regulation approach
- **Wahi Kaam Baar-Baar** — Duplicate effort
- **Cost Aur Team Burnout** — Budget khatam, log thak gaye
