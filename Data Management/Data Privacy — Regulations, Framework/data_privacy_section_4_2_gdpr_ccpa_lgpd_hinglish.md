### 4.2 GDPR vs CCPA vs LGPD — Similarities Hi Framework Ka Base Hain
**Har law ka apna thread, par core same**

Teen bade regulations ko side-by-side dekho to differences kam aur similarities zyada dikhti hain. Har law ka ek core "thread" hai — usko pakad lo to poora law samajh aa jaata hai.

### GDPR vs CCPA — Side By Side

**Similarities differences se kaafi zyada hain.**

| **Area** | **GDPR** | **CCPA** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Personal data samajhna | Haan — jo data aap hold karte ho | Haan — par **last 12 months** ke context me |
| Data subject rights (access, download) | Bahut strongly aligned | Bahut strongly aligned |
| Processing | Processing ke liye **reason / legal basis** chahiye | Processing hone ke baad log usse **opt out** kar sakein |
| Sold data | Explicit focus nahi | **Sold data** par bahut specific — yahi CCPA ka core thread hai |
| Transparency | Haan | Haan |
| Privacy notices | Third party ko bhi cover karta hai, kaafi explicit | Implicit basis par cover |
| Discrimination se protection | Nuanced / implied | **Explicitly** mentioned |
| Overall orientation | Processing ka legal basis + purpose limitation | Opting out + sold data transparency |

#### GDPR Ka Thread

- **"Processing ka legal basis kya hai?"**
- Data lene se **pehle** reason chahiye
- Purpose limitation
- Consent sirf ek legal basis hai — aur bhi hain

#### CCPA Ka Thread

- **"Last 12 months"** aur **"sold data"**
- Processing ke **baad** opt out ka haq
- Data bechna kis-kis ko
- Discrimination se explicit protection

**Yaad Rakhne Ka Tarika**

CCPA ka har element ghoom-phir kar "last 12 months" aur "sold data" par aata hai. GDPR ka har element "legal basis for processing" par aata hai. Ye do threads yaad rakho to dono easily differentiate ho jaate hain.

### Brazil Ka LGPD Aur Wo Common Themes Jinse Framework Banta Hai

**LGPD GDPR ke aur bhi zyada kareeb hai.**

Brazil ka data privacy law (**LGPD**) GDPR ke aur bhi kareeb hai — CCPA se bhi zyada. Lagbhag har major category same hai.
