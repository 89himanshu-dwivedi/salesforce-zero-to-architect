# 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation

## 2.1 Pillar 3 ka main idea

Customer ko sirf privacy policy ka link dena enough nahi. Use samajh aana chahiye:
- Kaunsa personal data collect ho raha hai?
- Kis purpose ke liye use hoga?
- Kin categories ya named vendors/recipients ke saath share hoga?
- Kaunse choices available hain?
- Marketing preferences kaise change ya withdraw kar sakta hai?
- Applicable law ke under access, correction, deletion ya doosre rights kaise exercise karega?

**Transparency + usable controls = trust.** Lekin transparency ka matlab sirf long policy ko short bullet list mein badalna nahi. Information accurate, specific, accessible aur applicable law ke mutabik complete bhi honi chahiye.

## 2.2 Fake phone number wali kahani

Agar form phone number maangta hai aur user ko reason nahi batata, user fake number de sakta hai. Field technically complete ho gayi, lekin business ke liye data inaccurate hai.

Better design:
- Agar phone number genuinely optional hai, to optional rakho.
- Agar required hai, to usi form ke paas clear reason batao.
- Purpose ko real use ke saath match rakho.
- Agar number 2FA ke liye hai, to explain karo ki authentication ke liye use hoga.
- Agar delivery issue ke liye contact karoge, to woh purpose bhi clearly disclose karo.
- “Kisi partner ko nahi denge” jaisi guarantee tabhi do jab woh sach, current aur vendor/data-sharing arrangements ke saath consistent ho.

**Important:** Transparency se accurate data milne ke chances improve ho sakte hain, lekin har user sahi data dega, iski guarantee nahi. Purpose explanation, optionality aur trust milkar data quality ko improve karne mein help karte hain.
