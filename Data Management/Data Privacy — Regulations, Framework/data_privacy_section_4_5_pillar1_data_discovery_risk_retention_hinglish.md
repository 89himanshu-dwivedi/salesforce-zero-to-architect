## 4.5 Pillar 1 — Apna Customer Data Dhoondo Aur Samjho
**Discovery, access, risk, minimise, retain**

Pehla pillar: data discovery, access samajhna, risk assess karna, minimisation, aur retention. Ye foundation hai — iske bina baaki dono pillars khade nahi ho sakte.

### Data Discovery — Kya Hai, Kahan Hai, Owner Kaun

**Sabse basic sawaal — par sabse zyada skip kiya jaane wala.**

📦

**Kya PII Hai**

Kaunsa personal data aapke paas hai.

📍

**Kahan Hai**

Kaunse system me reside karta hai.

👤

**Owner Kaun Hai**

Us data ka business owner kaun hai.

🛠️

**Manage Kaun Karta Hai**

Operationally kaun handle karta hai.

🤝

**Kis Ke Saath Share Hota Hai**

Partners, vendors, third parties.

**Example Inventory**

"Orders yahan hain, marketing list wahan hai, billing system yahan hai, support system wahan hai — aur in sab me us customer ka ek record hai, aur har ek ko koi na koi manage karta hai." Bas yahi likh lena discovery ka pehla step hai.

### Data Access Samajhna — Yahan Security Overlap Hoti Hai

**Access hona aur access hona chahiye — do alag sawaal.**

👀

**Kaun Dekh Raha Hai**

Is data ko abhi kaun access kar raha hai?

❓

**Kya Unhe Dekhna Chahiye**

Access hona aur access hona chahiye — dono alag sawaal hain.

🔗

**Kis Party Ke Saath Share / Sell**

Kis partner ko share ya sell kar rahe ho.
