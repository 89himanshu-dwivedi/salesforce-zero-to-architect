# 3.2.1 Business Kya Hai Aur Kya Consolidate Ho Raha Hai

## Distributed Sales Model — Direct Nahi, Dealer Ke Through

Clean Bikes ka sales model **direct sales nahi** hai.

```text
Clean Bikes
     |
     | Manufactures
     v
Zero-Emission Motorcycles
     |
     v
Dealerships
     |
     | Sell motorcycles
     v
End Customers
```

---

## Clean Bikes Ka Sales Model

### 🏭 Clean Bikes

**Zero-emission motorcycles banati hai.**

### 🏪 Dealerships

**Exclusively inhi ke through bikri hoti hai.**

### 🧍 End Customers

**Dealer se motorcycle kharidte hain.**

### Business Flow

```text
             CLEAN BIKES
                  |
                  v
       Zero-Emission Motorcycles
                  |
                  v
             DEALERSHIPS
                  |
                  v
           END CUSTOMERS
```

### Architect Ke Liye Important Point

Ye **B2B2C model** jaisa behave karta hai:

```text
B = Clean Bikes
B = Dealer
C = End Customer

Clean Bikes
     |
     v
  Dealer
     |
     v
 Customer
```

Iska direct impact aayega:

- Account model
- Contact model
- External user model
- Partner/dealer portal
- Security
- Sharing
- Licensing
- Sales process
- Customer service
- Data ownership
- Reporting
- Integration
