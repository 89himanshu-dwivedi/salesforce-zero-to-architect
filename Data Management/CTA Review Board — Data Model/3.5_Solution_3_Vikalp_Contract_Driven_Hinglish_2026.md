# 3.5 Solution 3 (Vikalp) — Contract-Driven Model
## Contract = Agreement + Customer Info Object

> **Source-preservation:** Supplied 3.5 ke saare major points preserve kiye gaye hain. Current 2026 Salesforce validation/corrections ko alag section mein rakha gaya hai.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Vikalp ka architecture **Contract-driven** hai:

- **Contract = agreement**; Opportunity ko actual agreement nahi maana gaya.
- **Tax ID = separate Customer Info custom object** mein isolate kiya gaya.
- Reason: Account regional sharing ke through expose ho sakta hai, jabki Tax ID ko restricted rakhna hai.
- Dealer motorcycle sales aur direct accessory/spare-parts sales dono model kiye gaye hain.
- Service side par **Product Registration → Service Contract/Entitlement → Cases + Knowledge** ka concept hai.
- High-volume Error Codes ko raw records ke bajay **summarisation** ke through reporting ke liye model kiya gaya hai.

# 3. Do Tarah Ki Sales

Kuch products company directly bechti hai:

- Accessories
- Spare parts

Motorcycles dealers bechte hain.

Isliye customer ke liye **Order** object bhi model kiya gaya.

```text
Clean Bikes
 ├── Motorcycles → Dealers → Customers
 └── Accessories/Spare Parts → Direct Company Sale → Customers
```

# 4. Standard vs Custom

Vikalp ne diagram ko explicitly **Standard** aur **Custom** sections mein divide kiya.

Principle:

> **Jitna possible ho standard Salesforce objects use karo; custom object tab banao jab business/security requirement justify kare.**

## 4.1 Standard Objects

- Lead
- Account (partner accounts)
- Contact
- Contract + Contract Line Items
- Opportunity
- Price Book / Price Book Entry
- Order
- Asset
- Case + Entitlement
- Knowledge

## 4.2 Custom Objects / Settings

- **Contract terms & legal language** — custom setting
- **Discount / special reason** — regional threshold
- **Customer Info** — Tax ID isolate karne ke liye
- **Product Registration** — customer khud register karta hai
- **Error Code** summarisation
