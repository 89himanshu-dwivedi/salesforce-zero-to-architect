# 1. Executive Summary

Board-level Q&A mein sirf “ye feature available hai” bolna enough nahi hota.

Real question hota hai:

> **“Tumne ye design choice kyu li? Dusra option kyu nahi? Cost, capability, security aur maintainability ka trade-off kya hai?”**

Is section mein major debates:

1. Dealers → Partner Community vs Full Sales Cloud
2. Sharing Set vs Sharing Rules
3. Chatter External vs Customer Community + Chatter
4. Annual Agreement → Contract vs Opportunity
5. Monthly Quantity Schedule → Custom Object vs Standard Quantity Schedule
6. CSR → Service Cloud vs Sales Cloud + Cases

---

# 2. Board Mindset — Trade-off Dikhao

Interview/board mein ideal answer structure:

```text
Requirement
    ↓
Option A
    ↓
Benefits
    ↓
Limitations / Cost
    ↓
Option B
    ↓
Benefits
    ↓
Limitations / Cost
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Why this decision fits the requirement
```

### Golden Rule

> Agar presentation ke beech mein realize ho ki initial choice ideal nahi thi, to usko honestly acknowledge karo aur trade-off explain karo.

Data model todne se better hai:

> **Reasoning + trade-off + business impact explain karna.**

Judges justification ko value karte hain.

# 3. Bahas 1 — Dealers Ko Partner Community Ya Full Sales Cloud?

## Classic Trade-off

> **Cost vs Capability**

Clean Bikes ke paas **12,000+ dealer users** hain, isliye license choice ka financial impact significant hai.

---

# 4. Option A — Partner Community (Nikki)

### Advantages

- **Lower cost**
- 12,000+ dealer users ke scale par major cost difference
- Opportunity access mil sakta hai
- Sharing rules / sharing sets architecture mein use ho sakte hain, subject to license/object capability
- External sellers ke liye natural fit

### Business Model

```text
Clean Bikes
     │
     ▼
Dealer Account
     │
     ├── Owner
     ├── Sales Manager
     └── Sales Team
           │
           ▼
     Partner Community
```

Partner Community specifically B2B partner experiences aur sales-data access use cases ke liye designed hai. citeturn0search8turn0search10

---

# 5. Option B — Full Sales Cloud (Susannah)

### Advantages

- Broader internal-sales capabilities
- Agar dealers Clean Bikes ke saath **bahut closely** kaam karte hain, higher capability justify ho sakti hai.
- More internal-style sales functionality available ho sakti hai.

### Disadvantage

- **Significantly higher cost** at 12,000+ users.
- External partner sellers ke use case mein full internal license over-provisioning ho sakta hai.

Presenter ne khud acknowledge kiya tha ki partner community better fit ho sakta hai.
