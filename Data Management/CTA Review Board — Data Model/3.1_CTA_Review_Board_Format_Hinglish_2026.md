## 3.1.1 Session Ka Format Aur Review Board Ke Ground Rules

Ye ek CTA prep practice session hai jisme teen log ek hi scenario ka solution present karte hain.

Yahan format, timing aur review board ke asli rules samjho — kyunki practice ko board jaisa banana hi maqsad hai.

### Core Flow

```text
Scenario
   |
   v
Solution Preparation
   |
   v
Architecture / Data Model / Security Design
   |
   v
Presentation
   |
   v
Judge Questions
   |
   v
Justification + Trade-offs
   |
   v
Scoring / Evaluation
```
